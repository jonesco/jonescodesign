Flatbed for macOS

Privacy, kept simple.

Flatbed is designed as a personal workspace. Jonesco does not collect your issue content, track you, or sell your information.

Data collection

Flatbed does not collect personal data, analytics, advertising identifiers, diagnostics, or usage history for Jonesco. The app has no account system and does not send your work to a Jonesco-operated server.

Your issue content

Titles, notes, checklist items, links, statuses, and timestamps are stored on your Mac. When iCloud is available, Flatbed may sync this content through your private iCloud database so it is available on your other Macs. Apple operates iCloud and processes that data under your Apple account and Apple’s privacy terms. Jonesco cannot view the contents of your private iCloud database.

Search and preferences

Search happens locally in the app. Flatbed may store basic preferences, such as window layout, on your device. These are not transmitted to Jonesco.

Backups

Import and export use JSON files at locations you select. Flatbed does not upload exported backups. A backup remains wherever you saved or copied it until you delete it.

External links

Links you save in an issue open in your default browser. Flatbed does not fetch or inspect those destinations. Once opened, the destination site’s privacy practices apply.

Retention and deletion

Deleting an issue removes it from Flatbed and, when iCloud sync is active, syncs that deletion. Removing the app may not by itself remove data retained in your private iCloud account. Exported backups must be deleted separately from the locations where you saved them.

Children

Flatbed is a general productivity tool and is not directed to children. Because Jonesco does not collect data through Flatbed, the app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If Flatbed’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes take effect and the App Store privacy information will be revised when required.

Contact

Questions about Flatbed privacy can be sent to wes@jonesco.com.
